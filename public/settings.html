Network participation
Controls whether devices verified by your app contribute to and benefit from the cross-app reputation graph.
Opt out of Vouchflow Network
By default, devices contribute verification history to the shared reputation graph, giving you better confidence signals at scale. Enable this to exclude your app's devices from the network. Requires user consent in your app to participate.
